About

Linux developer from Ukraine.

I build Simple Linux, a small distribution on top of Gentoo, and the tools around it, mostly in Rust: an installer, an app installer, an app store for Portage, terminal helpers and a screen streamer.

My own machine is a gaming laptop running Gentoo and OpenRC that I tune myself, from the kernel build to power and GPU clocks. Most of my tools started as something I needed there, and I keep notes and measurements of what worked and what did not.

I also work with BreezyOps Development Labs, the company of Daniel Robbins, creator of Gentoo and Funtoo: testing, bug reports and fixes for its projects.
