About

Linux developer from Ukraine. I build a Gentoo-based distribution and the tools around it, mostly in Rust.

I build Simple Linux, a small distribution on top of Gentoo: the live images, the installer, the kernels it deploys, the binary packages it installs from and the desktop it boots into.

Around it are the desktop tools it needed: an app store for Portage, a Windows-style app installer, terminal apps that act like desktop apps and a Wayland screen streamer.

My own machine is a gaming laptop running Gentoo that I tune myself, from the BIOS and kernel build to power limits and GPU clocks. Most of my tools started as something I needed there, and I keep notes and measurements of what worked and what did not.

I also work with BreezyOps Development Labs, the company of Daniel Robbins, creator of Gentoo and Funtoo: testing, bug reports and fixes for its projects.

What I work on

  • Distribution building

    Live ISOs, stage3, an installer with a resumable 16-phase pipeline, signed binary packages, release automation.

  • Desktop apps

    GTK4 and libadwaita apps in Rust that share one look and one language setting, plus terminal frontends on the same cores.

  • Kernels

    Configs for thousands of hardware combinations from Kconfig fragments, and Clang builds with LTO, AutoFDO and Propeller profiles.

  • Hardware tuning

    Undervolting, power limits, EC and fan control, deep idle on battery, GPU clock offsets on Wayland.

  • Testing upstream

    End-to-end tests of other people's tools on real roots and other architectures, with small, focused pull requests.

How I work

  • Say what was tested

    Every README says what was tested, how, and what was not. Numbers come from measurements, with the machine named.

  • Show it

    Every project has screenshots of the real program, so you can see what you get before installing.

  • One family

    Programs look alike and share one language setting, English by default.

  • Built from need

    Tools start as fixes for problems on my own machine and grow from there.
